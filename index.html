About Me

I specialize in transforming concepts into precise, production-ready designs that meet rigorous real-world performance requirements. My work involves managing complex assemblies, conducting structural validation, and performing fluid flow analysis to ensure reliability, efficiency, and durability.

I approach every project with an uncompromising engineering mindset—balancing sleek design, raw functionality, and practical manufacturability. Leveraging industry-standard tools including Siemens NX, Creo Parametric, Ansys, and Fusion 360, I architect and optimize mechanical systems from the ground up.

I am continuously refining my technical skill sets and taking on challenging projects to deliver efficient, practical, and incredibly high-performance engineering solutions.
